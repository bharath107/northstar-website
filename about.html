About Northstar.

The route to Germany is very doable, but the information is scattered across agents, forums and WhatsApp groups. We bring it into one clear plan, with people on the ground who know the system.

Why Northstar exists

Too many talented doctors lose a year or more to the wrong state, a missing document or an exam they took too early. Others pay for help that ends the day the visa arrives.

Northstar stays with you from your first German class to your first months in a German hospital. A north star does not walk the road for you; it makes sure you never lose your direction.

  • Built only for Indian doctors
  • Founders who made the move themselves
  • One point of contact throughout
  • Honest advice, no false promises

How we work

A plan before a payment

Your free consultation ends with a clear plan, whether or not you join us.

Realistic timelines

We tell you what is likely, not what sounds good. Surprises cost you time and money.

Support after arrival

Registration, housing, Hospitation and your first weeks at work are part of the job.

Small groups

We take on a limited number of doctors at a time so everyone gets proper attention.

The founders

Meet the team

Two co-founders from Bangalore who made the move to Germany themselves, and now help doctors do the same.

Sahel Anees

Co-founder and Student Success Lead

Sahel brings a social worker’s eye to the move abroad: a deep understanding of people, systems and the practical realities of settling in Germany. Originally from Bangalore, he has now been living in Germany for over five years.

He has navigated German bureaucracy himself, from the first form to the last stamp, and now guides others through it with clarity, patience and reliability, so that no one feels alone at any step of their journey.

Akash Kumar Sivakumar

Co-founder and Medical Pathway Lead

Born and raised in Bangalore, India, Akash moved to Germany in 2023 to pursue his professional journey in the healthcare sector. He holds an MBBS degree from India and has been working for over a year in a rehabilitation centre in Germany.

Through his own experience of the relocation process, Akash understands the challenges of moving abroad and is passionate about helping others make a smooth and confident transition to Germany.
