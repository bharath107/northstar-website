Why Germany

No USMLE marathon. No tuition for specialist training. Just a clear path and a salary from year one.

Why Indian doctors choose Germany

A long term need for doctors, and a system that pays you while you specialise.

Paid specialist training

Residency in Germany is a regular, salaried job. You are not paying to specialise; the hospital pays you.

Secure contracts

Most hospitals pay by collective agreement, with fixed salary steps, on call pay and strong employee rights.

Regulated working hours

Working time and rest periods are regulated by law, with generous paid leave every year.

Recognised across Europe

A German specialist qualification opens doors across the European Union.

What doctors earn in Germany

Monthly gross salaries in municipal hospitals under the TV-Ärzte VKA pay agreement, valid from June 2026. On call duty, night shifts and weekend work are paid on top.

Salaries at private, church run and university hospitals follow their own agreements and are usually similar. Chief physicians (Chefarzt) negotiate individually.
